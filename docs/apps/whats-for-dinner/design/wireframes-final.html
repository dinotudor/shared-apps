What's for dinner? in the SkiroLiro context

A mobile-first web app for the family to plan dinners: each day has a dish, a Skip dinner or a Free-style, and everyone responds, reacts and says what is missing. Useful first, with light humour and lots of emojis.

Base: the brainstorm handoff and earlier research (PRD and data model). The original wireframe canvas could not be opened.

Core idea

The app's sparkle comes from the family, not the interface: emojis, reactions and each day's responses. The interface stays calm, on a warm light background, and the logo (the thinking face) gives it personality. It appears in the header and in empty and loading states.

The 6 screens

1. Week7 days (dish, Skip, Free-style), reactions and a missing-ingredient badge. Empty day: “+ Planejar”.
2. DayPhoto, who planned it, who is fed up, ingredient alert, day notice, 4 responses, checklist, reactions.
3. Plan a daySkip dinner, Free-style or a saved dish, with search, average, times cooked and who is fed up.
4. DishesSearch, sort (most cooked, best score, recent), average, my score and Enjoei.
5. DishScore 0 to 10 with an emoji per band, Enjoei (3 weeks), family scores, ingredients and preparation.
6. New / editPhoto, name, ingredients, preparation. Save is primary. Delete with confirmation.

Product rules the design follows

  • “Order food” and “go out” create a notice on the day, without changing the plan.
  • Each person marks “Enjoei” on the dish screen. The notice shows for 3 weeks in Plan, Dishes and on the day.
  • Responses keep their original English text. A “soft” version comes later.
  • “Not home tonight” stays as “to be confirmed”.
  • The ingredient checklist starts fully checked. Unchecking shows the alert.

Planned Phase 2

Month (same day card, smaller), messages (3rd tab), statistics, shopping list (born from missing ingredients) and stickers on the dish photo. Icons already drawn.

Open questions

  • “Soft” version of the responses: per family or per person?
  • Reactions: a fixed list of emojis or any emoji?
  • Dish photos: where to store them (raised in earlier research).

Icons

  • Each file in icons/ is a 24 px SVG with stroke="currentColor": the colour comes from the surrounding text.
  • On the web, use the sprite: <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24"><use href="#i-week"/></svg> (include sprite.svg once on the page).
  • Sizes: 16 (inside chips), 20 (buttons) and 24 (navigation and rows). Stroke always 1.5 px.
  • An icon on its own needs an aria-label. In navigation, always with text.
  • Emojis: day responses, scores (🤢 😐 😍), reactions and Enjoei. Always with text next to them when they mean something (for example, “🤢 0 a 4”).

Flow examples

1. Plan a day

  1. Week: tap a “+ Planejar” day.
  2. Plan: choose Skip dinner, Free-style or search for a dish.
  3. Back on Week with the toast “Planejado: Lasanha de berinjela”.

2. Respond to dinner

  1. Week: tap the day. Day: tap one of the four responses (tapping again undoes it).
  2. If the response is order or go out, the day notice shows for everyone and the plan stays.

3. Missing ingredient

  1. Day: uncheck an ingredient.
  2. The amber alert “Ingrediente faltando! Quem vai? 😄” appears with the “Eu vou” button.
  3. On tap, the alert becomes a green confirmation with “Desfazer”.

4. Score and mark Enjoei

  1. Dish: tap a number from 0 to 10. The band (🤢 😐 😍) lights up.
  2. Tap “🤢 Enjoei”. The dish leaves your suggestions for 3 weeks and the notice shows in Plan, Dishes and on the day. It can be undone.

5. Create or edit a dish

  1. Dishes: “Novo prato” (or the pencil on Dish). Fill in name, ingredients (name and quantity) and preparation.
  2. “Salvar prato”. Delete asks for confirmation, saying what will be lost.

Tone rule: humour in responses, the ingredient alert, empty states and Enjoei. Never in errors, offline or delete.

Reactions

Any emoji. The list shows the most used ones, and the text field accepts any emoji from the phone keyboard. The row icon shows the day's most popular reaction.

States and accessibility

  • Every data screen has empty, loading and error/offline states (the States board in the wireframes).
  • Touch targets of at least 44 px. State never by colour alone: always with text, an icon or an emoji.
  • All buttons in Play are real buttons, with visible focus and labels; it works by keyboard (Tab, Enter, Esc closes sheets and dialogs).
  • Light and dark mode follow the system; Play lets you switch manually.

Limits of this version

  • Play is a prototype: nothing is saved, and reloading the page resets the sample data.
  • Only the week of 9 to 15 November exists. Photos are dashed areas. Photo upload, sign-in and time zone are left to the implementation.
  • Phase 2 (month, messages, statistics, shopping list, stickers) has icons, but no screens.
