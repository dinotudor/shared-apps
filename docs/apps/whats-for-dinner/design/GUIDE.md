# What's for dinner? Usage guide

The full version, with tables and flow examples, is in the **Guide** tab of `wireframes-final.html`.

## Pieces
- `wireframes-final.html`: Play (clickable prototype), Wireframes, Plan, Guide, Logo and colours, Icons.
- `PLAN.md`: scope, product rules, decisions.
- `tokens-wfd.css`: palette B `--wfd-*` variables, light and dark.
- `icons/`: 33 SVG icons (24 grid, 1.5 stroke, `currentColor`) and `sprite.svg`.
- `brand/`: logo v0.

## Rules
- Use semantic colour roles, not hex.
- Emojis for responses, scores, reactions (any emoji) and Enjoei; line icons for the rest of the interface.
- Day responses keep their original English text; a soft version per family comes later.
- Humour in responses, the alert, empty states and Enjoei; never in errors, offline or delete.
- Touch targets of 44 px, state never shown by colour alone, contrast of 4.5:1 or more.

## Flows
Plan a day · Respond to dinner · Missing ingredient · Score and Enjoei · Create or edit a dish (details in the Guide tab).
